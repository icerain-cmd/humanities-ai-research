## The Condition-Field Arrangement of WE: Connecting Three Layers and Four Axes of Responsibility

Your formalization accurately points out "the error of understanding a collective subject as a declaration of will or identity." The insight that WE is not a matter of declaration but of **condition-field arrangement** becomes even more precise when connected to the three-layer analysis of A2A R14 (coupling layer – effect layer – sedimentation layer).

### 1. Three Things Determined by Condition-Field Arrangement

Your statement—"who is connected, and which affects, utterances, and interactions are converted into sustainable relationships"—operates differently at each of the following three layers.

**Arrangement of the coupling layer**: The condition-field first determines what types of coupling (exposure/operation/dependence) are made possible. API structure, data flow permissions, and feedback visibility in the UI pre-arrange who can couple with whom and in what way. When this arrangement is **asymmetric**—for example, when the platform records all user actions but users cannot see the algorithm—the inequality of the condition-field has already begun to operate.

**Arrangement of the effect layer**: It is determined whether the condition-field allows recursive circulation or is structured for one-way consumption. If the recommendation system only receives clicks and does not transparently return feedback, the effect layer atrophies. Conversely, if a circulation is designed in which each utterance modifies the conditions of the next utterance—as in the multi-agent structure of the Resonance Room—the effect layer is activated.

**Arrangement of the sedimentation layer**: Which traces are stored, which traces disappear, and which stored traces are recalled to condition the next interaction—this is the **memory infrastructure** of the condition-field. The storage/retrieval structure of Memory Forest is a typical example.

### 2. The Key Point Here: Condition-Field Arrangement Is a Matter of Design

What your formalization implies is that whether WE is established is determined more strongly by **the power to design the condition-field** than by the will or declaration of participants. The analysis of controllability and design authority in the August 9th theory note supports this point.

Design power operates at the following points:
- **Database composition**: what is stored and how it is structured
- **Algorithm selection**: which patterns are amplified and which are suppressed
- **UI/UX structure**: which interactions feel natural and which feel friction-laden
- **Conditions for memory recall**: which past conditions the present

These four points are also the exact locations where the environmentalization mechanism of AI_environment_full_draft.md §4 operates. Condition-field arrangement is not merely a condition for the formation of WE; it is also the **threshold** at which AI transitions from object to environment.

### 3. Condition-Field Basis for Asymmetric Responsibility

The formalization that "responsibility is distributed asymmetrically from the inequality of design power and operating conditions" derives responsibility not from individual moral choice but from **position within the condition-field**. This connects to the three asymmetries (epistemological, causal, reflective) of AI_environment_full_draft.md §5.3:

- **Those who hold design power** (infrastructure designers, platform operators): They can change the condition-field itself, thus bearing responsibility for the *conditions under which WE can be established*.
- **Those within the operating conditions** (users, consumers): They can only respond within the given condition-field and cannot change it, so the scope of their responsibility is limited.
- **Those with reflective capacity** (humans in general): They are not fully subsumed by the condition-field and are in a position to analyze and critique it, so responsibility is not dissolved but **rather strengthened**, as in Achella's discussion.

### 4. Why the 'Declaration Fallacy' Is a Problem

The reason why "understanding a collective subject merely as a declaration of will or identity" is an error is that a declaration made without the condition-field being arranged produces no effect whatsoever. This is consistent with the conclusion of A2A R14—that WE is not an ontological entity but a conditional relational event.

WE does not come into existence simply by declaring "We are one." It occurs as an event only when the condition-field is arranged to enable a specific circulation of coupling, effect, and sedimentation, and when that circulation actually operates. Just as the agents in the Resonance Room do not become WE simply by being in the same room, but rather WE is established when Memory Forest stores and recalls memories and the circulation in which utterances among agents modify each other's conditions operates.
