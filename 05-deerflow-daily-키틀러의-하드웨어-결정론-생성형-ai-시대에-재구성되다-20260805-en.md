### III. Core Argument: Internal Tension of Kittler's Determinism and Its Reconstruction

#### 1. The Precise Structure of Kittler's Thesis

Kittler's determinism operates on two levels:

1. **Material Foundation (Hardware) Level**: Discourse is pre-determined by physical signal processing devices (GPU, silicon, electricity).
2. **Concealment/Ideology Level**: Software is a virtual layer that conceals this material foundation. Software "disguises material necessity as discursive contingency."

Now observe how these two levels are inverted in generative AI:

- **Hardware in the Kittlerian sense (GPU, silicon, transistors) remains the material foundation.** At this point, Kittler is partially correct. GPU parallel computation, memory bandwidth, and heat issues physically constrain the possibilities of the model.
- **However, what "determines" is not this hardware but the mathematical/software structures of weights, data, and embeddings.** Here, Kittler's thesis that software equals concealment ideology is directly challenged.

The core paradox is this: **What Kittler called "material a priori" in generative AI exists precisely at the software level.** Weight matrices, training distributions, and token embeddings — although physically stored in silicon — derive their determining power (determinacy) not from physical realization but from **statistical and topological structures**.

#### 2. The "Materiality" of Software Conditions — A Path for Reconstructing Determinism

The crucial insight here is not to refute Kittler but to **"transfer" his deterministic function to the software level**. What Kittler's determinism maintains is the proposition that "matter pre-structures discourse," not the equation "matter = hardware."

In generative AI, software conditions **acquire materiality themselves**:

- **Model Weights**: High-dimensional tensors composed of billions of parameters. This is not simply "code" but a statistical compression of training data and a **geometric crystallite**. Weights are a pre-prepared structure that probabilistically dominates specific outputs.
- **Data Distribution**: Pre-shapes the space of output possibilities. The statistical bias of the training corpus limits the "permissible space" of discourse produced, regardless of the prompt. This performs the same a priori role Kittler attributed to the typewriter's "mechanical selection."
- **Prompt Embedding**: The moment input is mapped into vector space, the user's question is already transformed into a vector subordinated to the model's topology. Embedding is the **inevitable point of concealment/structuring** where a "free question" is translated into the model's structural language.

These three elements are all **software-based but perform the same a priori function (pre-determining output) that Kittler attributed to hardware**. Therefore, Conclusion 1:

> **Kittler's deterministic functional structure remains valid in generative AI, but its "material foundation" has shifted from hardware to software (model structure, data distribution, embedding space).** Rather than refuting Kittler, the accurate reconstruction is to rename his thesis from "material a priori" to "computational a priori."

### IV. Synthetic Data Feedback Loops and Model Collapse: Evolution vs. Regression

#### (A) Regressive Closure Loop Interpretation (Kittlerian)

The phenomenon of model collapse strongly supports deterministic closure:

1. When a generative model reuses its own output as training data (synthetic data),
2. Each generation cuts off extreme values (tail distributions), reducing diversity,
3. Eventually, the model collapses into its own statistical average.

This **factually supports Kittler's closed determinism**. Model collapse is a phenomenon where a technical object becomes trapped in its own determining conditions (its own output), **losing openness**. This is structurally isomorphic to Kittler's claim that software conceals the closure of hardware. Recursivity here leads not to openness but to **pathological self-referential closure**.

In this interpretation, generative AI is the purest realization of Kittler's determinism: **The medium (its own training distribution) determines its own message, and this determination feeds back, converging into an increasingly rigid closure.**

#### (B) Evolutionary/Developmental Interpretation (Simondonian)

Conversely, Simondon's concretization reads as follows:

- A technical object is **not a discontinuous crystallite but a process**, and this process acquires internal coherence through "problem-solving."
- The synthetic data feedback loop (even if pathological) is a process where the technical object **interacts with its environment (its own output)** and reconfigures itself.
- Model collapse can be seen as **interrupted individuation** where concretization has not reached "complete individuation." In Simondon's terms, it is an incomplete state where the object has **not reached its point of internal resonance**.

#### (C) Which Interpretation is Supported? — Judgment

Theoretical judgment is needed here. The key is **whether the existence of model collapse itself supports either interpretation**.

- **The regressive closure interpretation is more strongly supported.** Model collapse is not simply a "technical defect" but **evidence that the evolutionary openness of a technical object is structurally blocked**. Simondon's concretization means a technical object **increases its organic coherence through interaction with its environment**, whereas model collapse is precisely the opposite — **loss of diversity and adaptability due to closed recursion with its own output**.

However, interestingly, **Simondon is not completely neutralized here**. Simondon's concretization does not guarantee evolution linearly; it implies **points of indeterminacy (the possibility of interrupted individuation)**. Within Simondon's framework, an object can remain in an "un-individuated potential state." Therefore:

> **Model collapse empirically supports Kittlerian closed determinism, while also being precisely explained as a case that violates the "normal individuation" criteria (simultaneous maintenance of internal coherence and environmental openness) presented by Simondon's concretization.** In other words, model collapse is a common verification point for both theories, and conclusively, **the regressive closure interpretation has higher explanatory power for this phenomenon.**

### V. Final Reconstruction: Transition to the Thesis of "Computational A Priori"

In summary, Kittler's proposition can be reconstructed for the generative AI era as follows:

**Original Thesis**: "The medium (hardware) determines the message — software is the ideology that conceals this determination."

**Reconstructed Thesis**: "Computational conditions (model weights, data distributions, prompt embeddings) determine the message — and this determination is not a concealable ideology but a structure possessing material reality in itself."

The difference is not merely terminological. There are three substantive implications:

1. **Shift of the Concealing Subject**: In Kittler, software conceals hardware. In generative AI, what conceals computational conditions is **the user interface (UI) and the natural conversational experience**. That is, "mechanical" concealment has shifted to "experiential" concealment. The user types a prompt, but the moment that prompt is subordinated to embeddings, weights, and distributions is completely transparent to the user.

2. **Modulatory (Circuit-like) Nature of Determination**: Kittler's determination was discrete (on/off, 0/1). Generative AI's determination is **probabilistic and distributional**. Strictly speaking, it is not "determination" but "sampling from a probability distribution." This softens determinism but **does not eliminate determinacy** — because the distribution itself is a pre-determined structure.

3. **Topological Change of Closure**: Kittler's closure was mechanical (hardware limits software). Generative AI's closure is **recursive (self-output limits self-training)**. Model collapse is a case where this recursive closure is pathologically maximized.
