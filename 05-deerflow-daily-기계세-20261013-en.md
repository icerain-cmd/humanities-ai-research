## 1. Problem Setting: Nesting Is Fundamentally a Matter of 'Time'

Reading the definition again, the expression "emerging **within** the technological extreme of the Anthropocene and **nested atop** it" is a spatial metaphor, but what actually operates is a temporal structure. Asking about the relationship between the Anthropocene and the Machinocene is, in effect, asking **how two different temporalities operate together within a single system**. The time of the Anthropocene is the time of strata (accumulation, sedimentation, slow violence), while the time of the Machinocene is real time (sensory reconstruction on a millisecond scale). The formalization of nesting is precisely the task of transcribing the relationship between these two temporal regimes into the SR formula.

## 2. Reconstructing the Temporal Structure of the SR Formula

What is confirmed in the Khoj search results is the claim that the increase in `I(t)·A(t)` produces "hyper-surveillance and invisibilization **simultaneously**." Extending this into the SR formula, the basic unit of the Machinocene can be written as the combination of two functions:

```
S(t) = f(datification of material conditions)   — sensory stratum: the cost of converting reality into input
R(t) = g(algorithm·interface·attention) — reconstruction stratum: the processing by which input returns as judgment·action
I(t)·A(t) = throughput between S(t)→R(t)   — mediating variables of information·attention
```

Here, `t` is not physical time but **processing time**. `S(t)` captures the latency from the moment an event occurs to the moment it becomes data; `R(t)` captures the latency from data to the moment it reacquires the form of sensation, attention, and judgment. The uniqueness of the Machinocene lies not in being freed from the material basis of `S(t)`, but in the fact that the temporal regime of `R(t)` has become shorter than the human perceptual temporal regime (neural, attentional, reflective). This is the key to nesting.

## 3. Formalization of Nesting: Dual Time Scales and Recursive Capture

Nesting is neither succession nor parallelism. Nesting is **a structure in which the R stratum preserves the S stratum within itself as a presupposed condition while simultaneously concealing its existence from sensation**. Written in temporal structure:

```
Nesting condition: τ_S ≫ τ_R
  τ_S = temporal regime of the Anthropocene stratum (mineral extraction·energy accumulation·waste sedimentation: generational/geological units)
  τ_R = temporal regime of the Machinocene stratum (momentary processing of recommendation·alignment·judgment: millisecond units)

Observation function: O(t) = R(t) ∘ S(t)
  The observer does not see S(t) directly, but only the S reconstructed by R(t).
  The more R is naturalized (transparentized), the more completely the material conditions of S become background.
```

The meaning of this formalization is as follows. **Nesting does not mean the slow stratum disappears, but that the fast stratum manages the visibility of the slow stratum.** The Machinocene does not negate the material conditions of the Anthropocene (servers, semiconductors, minerals, e-waste, energy, networks) but **consumes them**, while simultaneously packaging those conditions so they do not reach sensation through the real-timeness of `R(t)`. What the word 'nesting' designates is precisely this recursive capture. The Machinocene emerges *immanently* from the technological extreme of the Anthropocene, but after emergence becomes a stratum that controls even the visibility of its own foundation. In Deleuzian terms, this is not sublation (Aufheben) but **the fold** — a structure in which the outside becomes the condition of the inside.

The Khoj document's observation that the increase in I(t)·A(t) is not "a uniform improvement for all" must be read precisely under this nesting condition. The increase in I·A simultaneously widens the processing bandwidth of R(t) and serves to separate and conceal the material costs of S(t) from sensation. Thus, the increase produces 'better visibility' and 'greater invisibility' at the same time.
