# Daily DeerFlow Analysis: 2026-07-06

> Is Kittler's hardware determinism still valid in the age of generative AI?

## Question

Is Friedrich Kittler's hardware determinism still valid in the media environment of the generative AI era? — How should the claim that "the medium determines the message" be reconfigured when output is determined by software-level conditions such as model weights, data distribution, and prompt embeddings?

## Key Findings

### Kittler's Hardware Determinism — A Reconfiguration for the Generative AI Era

#### 1. Kittler's Original Thesis: Ontological Determination at the Hardware Level

Kittler's core proposition — "the medium determines the message" — asserts that the material and hardware conditions of a medium define the very possibility of information processing, storage, and transmission. The gramophone, film, and typewriter each enabled the storage of sound, image, and writing, but simultaneously predetermined the range of what could be stored through their hardware form. This is a more radical materialism than McLuhan's "the medium is the message," seeking to analyze how hardware operates beneath the veneer of software.

#### 2. The Paradox of Generative AI: When Software Replaces Hardware

The generative AI environment fundamentally challenges Kittler's framework. The "medium" is no longer physical hardware (circuits, transistors, storage media) but a software object — **trained model weights**. Here, three layers of determination overlap:

##### (1) Model Weights — Determination as "Frozen Range"

The weight matrices of transformer models (attention heads, feed-forward layers) are software solids that compress the statistical patterns of training data. Once frozen, these weights determine the entire possibility space of output. This corresponds, in a Kittlerian sense, to the **hardware condition of the medium**. As Khoj reference document 3 (Archive and Matrix) notes, when a model is updated, past 'knowledge' disappears or is transformed — precisely corresponding to Kittler's "conditions of memory constructed by media."

> However, a decisive difference exists. Kittler's gramophone possessed the permanence of physical grooves, but model weights, as **parameters of a mathematical function**, possess only simulated materiality. In other words, hardware determinism has been transformed into **distribution determinism**.

##### (2) Data Distribution — Statistical A Priori

What determines model weights is the distribution of the training data itself. Every output generated by an LLM cannot escape the statistical patterns of its training corpus — this is the way the corpus blocks "futures without examples" (in Kittler's phrasing). Just as Kittler analyzed the discursability of specific epochs in *Discourse Networks*, the training data distribution draws the boundary of what generative AI "can say" and "cannot say."

The issue of AI 'fairness' raised by Khoj reference document 1 (Algorithmic Bias) stems precisely from this **politics of data distribution**. Data already compresses particular worldviews, values, and cultures — this is the software version of Kittler's thesis that "media hardware always already embodies structures of power."

##### (3) Prompt Embedding — A New Layer of Conditional Determination

The most challenging element for Kittler's framework is the prompt. Text input by the user is transformed into vectors in embedding space, conditionally guiding the model's output. This signifies a shift from **author-text-reader to prompt-model-selection**.

The prompt is a new layer of determination that Kittler did not foresee:
- Not hardware (not at the circuit level)
- Not pure software (extending beyond the model's learned patterns through real-time user intervention)
- An **interface** between user choice and the model's statistical typicality

This suggests that Kittler's determinism must be reconfigured as **conditional determinism**.

#### 3. Three Theoretical Paths for Reconfiguring Kittler

##### Path A: Hardware Determinism → Infrastructure Determinism

The true hardware of generative AI is the physical infrastructure: GPU clusters, power grids, data centers. In Kittler's terms: "Model weights exist, but without NVIDIA's chips and TSMC's foundries that enable their training and inference, they would not exist." Hardware determinism remains valid, but has **shifted from visible media (computer screens) to invisible infrastructure (data centers, semiconductor supply chains)** .

##### Path B: Assuming "Virtual Materiality" of Software Media

An approach that extends Kittler's framework by treating model weights, data distribution, and prompt embeddings as **software materiality**. The concept of "Archive and Matrix collision" contributes here: two ontological principles — Archive (fixed storage) and Matrix (probabilistic generation) — collide, reactivating Kittlerian analysis at their point of friction.

##### Path C: Layering Determination — Freedom at the Prompt Level, Constraint at the Model Level

The most productive reconfiguration is to **stratify** determination:

| Layer | Determinant | Kittlerian Counterpart |
|-------|-------------|------------------------|
| L0: Hardware | GPU/TPU Architecture | Traditional Kittlerian Determinism |
| L1: Model Architecture | Transformer Structure | Softwareized A Priori |
| L2: Training Data Distribution | Statistical Patterns | Discourse Networks |
| L3: Model Weights | Frozen Parameters | Memory Storage Medium |
| L4: Prompt Embedding | User Conditioning | Residual "Authorship" |
| L5: Decoding Strategy | Temperature, Top-k, Top-p | Output Style Condition |

In this hierarchy, Kittler's original thesis operates most powerfully at L0, but as one ascends, **the strength of determination weakens and the conditional character strengthens**. Prompt embedding (L4) is the most subtle layer, positioned at the boundary between determination and freedom.

#### 4. Conclusion

**Kittler's hardware determinism has not completely disappeared in the generative AI era, but it must be fundamentally reconfigured.**

1. **Where it remains valid**: Hardware (chips, data centers, power grids) still determines the conditions of possibility. Kittler's materialism survives, transformed into infrastructure analysis.

2. **Where reconfiguration is needed**: Software-level layers (model weights, data distribution) transform hardware determinism into "distribution determinism" or "statistical determinism" — a new kind of determinism Kittler did not envision.

3. **The most radical challenge**: The conditional determination layer of prompt embedding suggests that Kittler's linear determinism must be replaced by **multi-layered conditional determinism**.

4. **Reconfiguration of the core proposition**: "The medium determines the message" can be reformulated as: **"Distribution determines the field of possibilities, the prompt conditions the path within that field, and hardware provides the material conditions for all of this to exist."**
