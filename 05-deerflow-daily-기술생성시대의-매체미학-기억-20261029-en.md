## Why 2023 Constitutes a 'Media-Historical Threshold': A Topological Shift in Generation and Recording

### 1. Setting the Problem: Functional Classification of Media and the Criterion for a Threshold

The question positions 2023 not as a mere 'technological event' but as a **media-historical threshold**. This criterion lies in the macro-level shift in the function of media from 'recording, storing, transmitting, and selecting' to 'co-performance of generation.' For historical comparison, it is necessary first to outline what functional axes each previous media transition transformed.

**Telegraph**: Compression of space. Separation of information from physical transportation. However, the content (message) of the telegraph remained entirely subordinate to the human symbolic system—the machine only transmitted encoded human language; it did not generate content.

**Radio**: Synchronization of time and one-way mass transmission. Radio performed only transmission without storage, and the receiver remained a passive listener. The medium spoke, and humans listened.

**Internet (especially Web 2.0)**: Addition of participation to the fusion of transmission and storage. Humans became both consumers and producers (prosumers), but what the internet performed was still the hosting, classification, and recommendation of human-made content. Algorithms automated arrangement and selection, but the **generation** of content remained the domain of humans.

All three of these transitions merely **mediated** human symbolic activities (language, images, concepts) but did not enter the interior of that activity itself. The medium remained 'outside' the process of human thinking, writing, and creation, limited to the role of storing, transmitting, and selecting the products.

### 2. The Topological Distinctiveness of Generative AI: From 'External Mediation' to 'Internal Co-Performance'

The transition of 2023 is qualitatively distinct from previous media transitions in the following three aspects.

#### 2.1. Algorithmization of Symbolic Production

Generative AI no longer transmits or classifies symbols produced by humans. Through the method of probabilistic next-token prediction, it **generates natural language, images, and code on its own**. This is a function that past media never performed.

The SOSS (Shape, Observe, Stir, Select) framework by Andersson & Elmqvist (2026) precisely captures this new relationship: humans **Shape** the AI as a creative medium, **Observe** its output, **Stir** it, and **Select** [citation:Andersson & Elmqvist 2026, "Material for Thought"]. Here, humans are no longer 'users' of the medium but become **dialogical co-performers** with an active creative medium.

The Texterial research team (Shen et al., 2026) expresses this relationship even more radically: text is no longer a transparent carrier of human thought but becomes a **material** that can be sculpted like clay or cultivated like a plant [citation:Shen et al., 2026, "Texterial"]. The medium has shifted from a tool that 'expresses' human thought to the material condition of thought itself.

#### 2.2. Internalization into the Creative Process

Unlike existing media (telegraph, radio, internet), generative AI is **internalized** into the creative process itself. As Khoj RAG Document 3 points out, generative AI is integrated into the learning process as a "tool that expands questioning, interpretation, and reflection." This signifies a **co-constitution** where human cognition and algorithmic generation intertwine within the generative process of creation, rather than the medium merely transmitting the work from 'outside.'

In the language of media theory, this difference goes beyond Friedrich Kittler's assertion that "the medium determines the message." For Kittler, the medium was still a condition of the human system of recording, storing, and processing, not a **partner that performs creation alongside humans**. After 2023, the medium is no longer a 'recording system' (Aufschreibesystem) but becomes a **generation system (Generierungssystem)** .

#### 2.3. Change in the Temporality of Creation: Instantaneous Co-Generation

Ding & Chan (2023) distinguish three interaction patterns in human-AI text co-creation: selection of content within a fixed scope, independent creation, and complex interdependent creation [citation:Ding & Chan, 2023, "Mapping the Design Space of Human-AI Text Co-creation"]. From 2023 onward, the third pattern (co-creation through iterative human-AI interaction) has become commonplace.

The implication is simple: the process of thinking and writing is no longer completed within the individual and then externalized, but **unfolds instantaneously within dialogical interaction**. This re-invokes, on a different level, the critique from Plato's *Phaedrus* where Theuth, regarding the invention of writing, speaks of "memory inscribed externally." Writing was an external repository of thought, but generative AI enables the **external unfolding** of thought itself. Thinking no longer occurs solely 'inside me' but 'between me and the medium' within the real-time responses of the algorithm.

### 3. Comparative Analysis with Previous Media Transitions

| Axis | Telegraph | Radio | Internet | Generative AI (2023-) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Function** | Space-compressing transmission | Time synchronization + one-way transmission | Storage, classification, participation | Co-generation |
| **Human-Medium Relationship** | Sender-Receiver | Broadcaster-Listener | Prosumer | Co-performer |
| **Subject of Content** | Entirely human | Entirely human | Entirely human | **Human + Algorithm** |
| **Externalization of Thought** | Encoding of messages | Synchronization of listening | Selective access to information | **Instantaneous external unfolding of thought process** |
| **Position of Medium** | External transmitter | External transmitter | External platform | **Internal co-constructor** |

The most prominent differences in the table above are the axes of **Subject of Content** and **Position of Medium**. All media before generative AI performed the transmission, storage, and selection of human-produced content. Generative AI, for the first time, occupies the position of an 'internal co-constructor' where the algorithm directly participates in the process of content production.

### 4. Contributions and Limitations of Khoj RAG References

**Contributions**: The topological approach (Generative Wonder → Unpleasant Strangeness → Critical Reflection → Relational Resonance) presented in Khoj Document 1 ("Argument for the Coherence of the Four Stages of Human-AI Contact") is valid in that empirical contact with generative AI has a phased transition structure. In particular, the first stage, 'Generative Wonder,' captures the public's initial reaction in 2023, and the final stage, 'Relational Resonance,' captures the development into a long-term co-creation relationship. The PLM concept from Khoj Document 5 (Definition of PLM), which "makes an individual's knowledge ecosystem and interpretive habits the operating conditions of the model," systematizes the process by which generative AI expands not just as a simple tool but as an individual's cognitive environment.

**Limitations**: These documents do not theorize the media-historical transition of 2023 itself. The category of 'Generative Wonder' is a description at the experiential level, not an analysis of the ontological shift in media function. Furthermore, a media-historical comparison (distinction from the telegraph, radio, and internet) is not addressed in these documents at all. Therefore, while the above analysis uses Khoj materials as empirical and phenomenological reference points, the core theoretical argument is constructed based on media theory and recent research such as Andersson & Elmqvist (2026), Shen et al. (2026), and Ding & Chan (2023).

### 5. Conclusion: The Meaning of the 2023 Threshold

The reason 2023 constitutes a media-historical threshold is that the following three conditions were simultaneously met:

**(1) Functional Transition of the Medium**: A shift from 'recording, storing, transmitting, and selecting' to 'co-performance of generation.' This is an unprecedented change in media history.

**(2) Internalization into the Creative Process**: The medium is no longer an external condition of creation but has become an internal component of the creative process itself.

**(3) Fusion with Everyday Cognitive Activities**: Generative AI has spread not merely as a tool for specialized domains (science, art) but across everyday thinking, writing, and creation.

The simultaneous fulfillment of these three conditions did not occur in any previous media transition, including the telegraph, radio, and internet. 2023 is the moment when the medium shifted from 'a tool that mediates human thought' to 'an environment that thinks with humans.' This transition is a type of extension that McLuhan, when defining the medium as "the extension of man," did not foresee—not a simple extension of sensory organs, but an algorithmic externalization and co-performance of cognitive generative capacity itself.
