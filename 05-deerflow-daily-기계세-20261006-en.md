# Analysis of 2024 U.S. Presidential Election Deepfakes and Platform Interventions

## The Dual Operation of Non-Human Agency in the Machine Apparatus — Production Device or Judgment Environment?

---

## 1. Problem Framework: Two Layers of Non-Human Agency

When analyzing together the candidate voice deepfakes that occurred during the 2024 U.S. presidential election and the platforms' responses, the non-human agency of the machine apparatus operates on two layers. One is a visible and narrow layer — its function as a **production device for false utterances**. The other is a more fundamental and structural layer — its function as a **judgment environment that rearranges the very conditions of authenticity, responsibility, and listening**.

The core argument of this analysis is as follows: the latter environmental dimension encompasses the former instrumental dimension, and in the 2024 election case, the most important non-human agency of the machine apparatus lies precisely in this **reconfiguration of the environment**. However, this environment is neither homogeneous nor monolithic; it is a **differentiated environment** where production, detection, mediation, and regulation collide.

---

## 2. The Machine Apparatus as Production Device — The Limited 'Object' Model

### 2.1 The Case of ElevenLabs and Voice Cloning

On January 21, 2024, two days before the New Hampshire primary, numerous voters received an AI-generated voice robocall that appeared to be President Joe Biden's voice. The message urged them to "save your vote for the November general election." Analysis confirmed the voice was produced using ElevenLabs' AI voice cloning technology[^1].

At this layer, the operation of the machine apparatus is clear:
- **Producer**: Political consultant Steve Kramer led the effort
- **Tool**: ElevenLabs' AI voice cloning (paid subscription $5/month)
- **Action**: Creation and dissemination of false utterances imitating a specific candidate's voice
- **Legal Outcome**: Kramer faced a $6M FCC fine and criminal charges for illegal robocalls and caller ID spoofing[^2]

In this model, AI is a **finite instrument (instrumentum)** . The human actor (Kramer) sets the goal, and the machine is the means to realize that goal. The trajectory of responsibility can be traced back to the human. This is an area manageable within the framework of traditional media ethics — 'the producer of the message bears responsibility.'

### 2.2 Limitations of This Model

However, this 'object model' fails to explain the following phenomena:

1. Although ElevenLabs blocked the account, the already disseminated content spread unverified across platforms.
2. ElevenLabs' voluntary blocking was not a moral choice but part of a **structure where the same AI infrastructure is used for both production and detection**. (ElevenLabs later partnered with Reality Defender to build a real-time detection system[^3].)
3. Even if responsibility is traced back to Kramer, the **effect of the deepfake's very existence altering the conditions of trust for all political speech** occurs independently of any individual actor's intent.

## 3. The Machine Apparatus as Judgment Environment — Core Argument

### 3.1 Fundamental Reconfiguration of Listening Conditions

The most fundamental change brought about by the introduction of deepfake technology into political communication is the transformation of the very **conditions of public listening**.

Borrowing from Kittler's concept of *Aufschreibesysteme* (discourse networks), media is not simply a channel for delivering content but constitutes the **material a priori** of what can be said, recorded, and disseminated in a given era[^4]. Just as Kittler noted that Foucault's discourse analysis stopped around 1850 because of the emergence of the phonograph, film, and typewriter[^5], the 2024 election marks a point where **AI-generated content + platform moderation algorithms + legal regulation** form a new discourse network.

The result of this environmental shift is as follows:

> **"All political audio is now presumed potentially fake."**

The New Hampshire incident was not merely a case of disinformation but an event where the **default setting** of public listening was transformed. A shift from the traditional model ('trust if it seems real') to a new model ('distrust unless proven fake') occurred across the entire environment through a single event. An NPR report accurately points out this phenomenon: "If Joe Biden says something in a speech, or Joe Biden says something to you in a robocall, you're told that you should dismiss them both"[^6]. This means the machine apparatus has fundamentally reconfigured the listening environment.

### 3.2 Algorithmic Intervention of Platforms — Operation as Judgment Infrastructure

The response strategies adopted by major platforms (Meta, YouTube, TikTok, X) for AI-generated content during the 2024 election show an important turning point:

| Platform | Policy | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Meta | "Made with AI" labeling, blocked 590,000 requests for election-related AI image generation[^7] | **Categorizes** AI content but chooses **labeling over removal** |
| YouTube/TikTok | Labeling AI-generated content, deletion only for 'significant harm' or election interference | Fact-checker flagged content receives **algorithmic demotion in feeds** |
| X | 'Misleading media' label applied | Minimalist response after withdrawing 17 existing disinformation protection policies |

What these policies commonly show is that while platforms **shift the ultimate responsibility for authenticity judgment to users**, they simultaneously **strengthen the algorithmic intervention** that performs this shift. Meta's **preemptive blocking** of 590,000 AI image generation requests for major candidates like Biden and Trump in the month before the 2024 election is a case where the pure non-human agency of the machine apparatus proactively constitutes the conditions of political speech[^7]. This is not simply 'censorship' but a **preemptive determination of the material conditions for what can be generated**.

In Kittler's terms, the platform's algorithm functions as a **technological a priori**. That is, whether a certain utterance reaches a listener's feed, gets labeled as AI-generated, or becomes entirely invisible is determined by **mechanical procedures** prior to any conscious human judgment.

### 3.3 Rearrangement of Responsibility — A Distributed Responsibility Environment

In the traditional media model, the chain of responsibility was linear: **producer → distributor → consumer**. In the environment of deepfakes + platform intervention, this chain is reconfigured as follows:

| Layer | Actor | Nature of Responsibility |
|-------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Production** | Political consultant (Kramer) | Legal/criminal responsibility (traceable) |
| **Tool Provision** | ElevenLabs | Account blocking, detection partnerships (voluntary/market-driven) |
| **Mediation/Moderation** | Meta, X, YouTube, etc. | Labeling/demotion/preemptive blocking (discretionary, non-uniform) |
| **Listening/Judgment** | Voters | Ultimate burden of verification (intentionally shifted by policy) |
| **Regulation** | FCC, judiciary | Ex-post sanctions (ban on AI voice in robocalls: Feb 2024) |

What is notable in this structure is that **responsibility is distributed across the nodes of the 'environment,' yet no single point can control the whole**. Meta labels, ElevenLabs blocks accounts, the FCC imposes fines, but **no one bears overall responsibility for the environmental effect of deepfakes fundamentally degrading trust in public discourse**.

This connects to the problem raised in my local Khoj reference notes on PLM research: "If personal data is biased or outdated, the PLM can automatically reproduce biases... Therefore, the PLM must not be a mere accumulation device for memory but a **verification structure** that includes source attribution, deletion criteria, and procedures for incorporating counterarguments"[^8]. Extending this insight, the platform environment itself is not a neutral pipeline but an operating environment that, through its **embedded verification structure (or lack thereof)** , constitutes the conditions for public reasoning.

### 3.4 The Same Infrastructure for Detection and Production — Asymmetry of the Environment

The most intriguing point is that **the detection infrastructure and the production infrastructure belong to the same machine apparatus**.

- ElevenLabs was the **production tool** for the Biden deepfake, and subsequently **partnered with Reality Defender to build a real-time detection system**[^3].
- Pindrop Security analyzed the Biden robocall and confirmed the use of ElevenLabs through **acoustic fingerprinting**[^9].
- Meta's restriction on AI image generation is a **block on production** using the same AI technology.

This symmetry has a critically important theoretical implication: **the machine apparatus is not a single 'object' but an environment where production and control, manipulation and verification all occur internally**. Just as Kittler said the phonograph stored sound while simultaneously redefining the material conditions of sound[^5], AI voice technology generates utterances while also technically redefining the definition of 'authentic utterance' itself.

## 4. Theoretical Synthesis — The Machine Apparatus as 'Environment' Rather Than 'Object'

### 4.1 Three Layers of Operation

Synthesizing the 2024 U.S. presidential election case, the non-human agency of the machine apparatus operates on three layers:

**① Surface Layer — Production and Dissemination of Utterances**
- Human actors use AI tools to produce false utterances
- The machine possesses limited, traceable agency as an **instrument (instrumentum)**
- Legal and institutional responses are possible (Kramer indictment, FCC fine)

**② Middle Layer — Infrastructure of Mediation and Moderation**
- Platform algorithms determine the visibility, labeling, and ordering of utterances
- The machine acts as a **judgment infrastructure**
- Humans must passively accept the output of this environment or actively circumvent it (algospeak)

**③ Deep Layer — A Priori Reconfiguration of Conditions for Authenticity, Listening, and Trust**
- The very existence of deepfakes changes the conditions of trust for all political speech
- The listening frame "all audio is potentially fake" becomes the default setting
- At this layer, the agency of the machine apparatus operates as an **environmental condition that transcends individual events**

### 4.2 Differentiation and Conflict of the Environment

Importantly, this environment does not operate uniformly or under a single logic. The 2024 case shows the conflict of a **differentiated environment (dispositif)** :

1. **Logic of Production**: ElevenLabs' voice cloning — low cost, high fidelity, exponential scalability
2. **Logic of Blocking**: ElevenLabs' account blocking, Meta's 590K image generation blocks — ex-post, incomplete
3. **Logic of Labeling**: Meta's "Made with AI" — shifting responsibility under the guise of informational neutrality
4. **Logic of Demotion**: Moving fact-checker flagged content to the bottom of feeds — invisible censorship
5. **Logic of Regulation**: FCC $6M fine, criminal indictment — limits of ex-post, punitive response
6. **Logic of the Listener**: An environment where users must suspect all content — cognitive overload

These five (or six) logics conflict within the same machine apparatus, and no single one dominates the whole. This is precisely why the machine apparatus must be defined as an **environment rather than an object**. An object is controllable, has boundaries, and is subordinate to a specific purpose. But an environment is a field where various forces collide from within, and no one controls the entire environment.

### 4.3 Acknowledging the Partial Validity of the 'Object' Model

This analysis does not completely deny the 'object' model. Rather:

- At the **legal level**, the object model remains valid. Kramer's indictment and fine show that the traditional framework of attributing responsibility still operates.
- At the **production level**, AI was used as a tool, and this instrumentality cannot be ignored.
- For **short-term, narrow analysis**, the object model has practical explanatory power.

The problem is that this object model **misses the most important effect of the machine apparatus — the change in the conditions of the entire environment**. Although the Biden deepfake robocall ended as a single event, the change in listening conditions it triggered persisted through the November 2024 general election and has become a new condition that platforms and voters must contend with in all future elections[^6].

---

## 5. Conclusion: Beyond the Production Device of Utterances, to the Judgment Environment

When analyzing together the candidate voice deepfakes of the 2024 U.S. presidential election and the platforms' real-time interventions, the non-human agency of the machine apparatus **does not remain merely a production device for false utterances; it operates as a judgment environment itself that rearranges the conditions of utterance authenticity, political responsibility, and public listening.**

The characteristics of this 'environmental turn' can be summarized as follows:

1. **Non-linearity**: Production, dissemination, judgment, and responsibility are not a linear chain but are constituted cyclically within the infrastructure. The same machine apparatus (e.g., ElevenLabs) is used for both production and detection.

2. **Apriority**: The agency of the machine apparatus is exerted more powerfully in the **preemptive setting of conditions for what can be generated, disseminated, and listened to** than in ex-post judgment of individual content.

3. **Distributed responsibility**: Responsibility is distributed among producers, intermediaries, listeners, and regulators, but a **responsibility gap** emerges regarding the effect of the environmental shift (change in trust conditions), for which no one is held accountable.

4. **Irreversibility**: Once deepfakes are introduced into the political listening environment, it is impossible to return to a pure state of trust 'before deepfakes.' The environmental shift is a structural change that cannot be reduced to the removal of a single event.

5. **Coherence as a differentiated environment**: Five conflicting logics (production, blocking, labeling, demotion, regulation) collide within a single field, but this collision itself is the constitutive principle of the environment.

In conclusion, the non-human agency of the machine apparatus extends beyond what the 'object model' of a disinformation production device can capture, operating in a deeper and more structural way as an **environment that redefines the ontological conditions of political communication itself**. Within this environment, the meanings of authenticity, responsibility, and listening are no longer given but are newly constituted each time by mechanical procedures. This is the most fundamental non-human agency of the machine apparatus revealed by the 2024 U.S. presidential election case.
