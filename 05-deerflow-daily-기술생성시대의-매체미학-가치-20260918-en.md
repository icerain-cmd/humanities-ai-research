**(a) Originality (Originality/Aura)** — To borrow Benjamin's argument, the artwork possesses a 'unique existence' occurring within a specific spatiotemporal context, and its 'aura' is lost through technological reproduction. Khoj reference 4 records this point as "the changes that reproduction technology has brought to human perceptual and experiential structures," reminding us that Benjamin envisioned a new social function for art through this transformation.

**(b) Intentionality** — The meaning of a work is defined by the subjective intentions of the human creator, and the audience stands in a position of deciphering that meaning.

**(c) Scarcity** — The original is unique, and this uniqueness provides the economic foundation of value.
