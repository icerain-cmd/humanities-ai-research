# Haraway's 'God Trick' and Generative AI: An Epistemological Stress Test

## Introduction: The Epistemological Terrain of the Problem

Donna Haraway's critique of the 'god trick' in her 1988 essay *Situated Knowledges* went beyond simple science criticism — it was an exposure of the **concealment mechanism of locatability** in knowledge production. Western modern science, while purporting to occupy a neutral, omniscient observer's vantage point, actually naturalized knowledge produced from a very specific historical and material position (the Western white male heterosexual subject) as **universal truth**. This is the core of the god trick.

Haraway's alternative was clear: all knowledge is **partially determined** by the producer's specific location, and therefore genuine objectivity is achieved through the connection of **partial perspectives**. The responsibility of knowledge depends on **locatability** — revealing where one speaks from.

Now apply this framework to the contemporary technological condition of generative AI. The question's core stands at a crossroads:

1. Is generative AI's 'view from everywhere' a **sophisticated upgrade** of the god trick?
2. Or does it represent a **fundamentally new condition of knowledge production** that Haraway's epistemological framework **cannot capture**?

---

## 1. The Epistemological Mechanism of Generative AI: 'The Statistical God'

The training process of large language models (LLMs) exhibits the following epistemological characteristics:

### 1.1 Constitutive Bias of Training Data — Structured Imbalance

Training data for GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, etc., relies primarily on **Common Crawl** web-scraping data. The composition of this data shows:
- **Linguistic imbalance:** English comprises approximately 60-70% of all data. Non-English languages like Korean, Japanese, and Arabic occupy negligible proportions, causing the model to naturalize **English's epistemological frame** as the basic technical language of the world.
- **Cultural imbalance:** Web data is skewed toward Western (especially North American and European) internet users and platform ecosystems. Major data sources like Reddit, Wikipedia, GitHub, and news sites reflect specific cultural, class, and educational backgrounds.
- **Gender imbalance:** Both producers and consumers of web content exhibit gender imbalances, which become structured as **discursive bias** in the training data.

These imbalances are not mere 'noise' — they are **the condition of the training data itself**. The model learns not from 'unbiased' data, but from a **specific distribution of biases**.

### 1.2 Statistical Averaging — The Mechanism of 'Seeing Everywhere'

When an LLM generates a response to a specific prompt, it undergoes the following process:
1. **Distributional center estimation:** Selecting the token sequence with the highest probability in the training data's probability distribution
2. **Outlier elimination:** Specific local perspectives, minority positions, and non-mainstream expressions are treated as statistical 'noise' and eliminated during averaging
3. **Surface neutrality:** The result of taking the 'statistical average' of all perspectives generates neutral-seeming responses that **belong nowhere yet appear valid everywhere**

This is the mechanism of 'view from everywhere.' The model does not speak from a specific location — it speaks from the **statistical center of all locations**. But this center is **not naturally given — it is determined by the constitutive biases of the data**.

### 1.3 Generation of 'Surface Responses' — Transparent Friction

The concept of 'transparent friction' from the research document *Ecology of Friction in the Age of Technical Generation* provides important insight: "The practicality of transparent friction is not merely a matter of individual human capacity. It is also a matter of designing non-human systems. How do designed systems block transparent friction and generate surface responses?"

LLMs are designed to generate **frictionless responses** — not arguments for a specific perspective, but 'safe' statistical averages that all perspectives can agree upon. This provides users with a **frictionless knowledge consumption experience**, but simultaneously eliminates epistemic responsibility through the **erasure of positionality**.

## 2. Upgrade of the God Trick, or a New Condition?

Three possible interpretations are presented.

### 2.1 Continuity Thesis: Sophisticated Upgrade of the God Trick

According to this position, generative AI is the **most sophisticated form** of the god trick:
1. **Deepened concealment of positionality:** In traditional science critiqued by Haraway, the researcher's location (institution, funding source, cultural background) was partially traceable. LLM training data composition and model weights, however, are **black-boxed**, making it nearly impossible to trace which biases operate through which mechanisms.
2. **Fortified universalism:** While traditional science claimed a 'God's-eye view,' LLMs claim **'every human's viewpoint.'** The narrative — "this model learned from data produced by billions of humans" — provides a far more powerful legitimization mechanism than existing universalism.
3. **Elimination of partiality:** The condition of 'responsible knowledge' that Haraway emphasized — partiality — is systematically eliminated in LLM responses. Models prefer ambivalent responses ("on one hand A, on the other hand B"), which functions as a mechanism for avoiding **political responsibility** for taking a specific position.

### 2.2 Discontinuity Thesis: New Condition of Knowledge Production

This position argues that generative AI has created **entirely new conditions** that Haraway's epistemological framework cannot capture:
1. **Scale beyond human epistemology:** Haraway's epistemology presupposes the **human subject's** situated knowledge. LLMs, however, are based on statistical pattern recognition independent of human epistemological categories (intentionality, embodiment, historicity). The model generates text based on probabilistic distribution without 'belief' or 'intent.'
2. **New form of distributed knowledge:** LLM knowledge does not originate from a **specific subject's position** but **emerges from statistical relationships among billions of texts**. This is a new form of **distributed knowledge** that is neither a 'God's-eye view' nor a 'partial perspective.'
3. **Dissolution of the epistemological subject:** Haraway's critique of the god trick demands a **better subject** (situated, partial, responsible). But LLMs suggest the **dissolution of the epistemological subject concept itself**. When knowledge becomes a property not of subjects but of **networks and distributions**, Haraway's framework demands fundamental reexamination.

### 2.3 Dialectical Thesis: Appropriation and Transformation of the God Trick

This position acknowledges both continuity and discontinuity, arguing that generative AI **appropriates while also transforming** the god trick:
1. **Structural similarity:** LLM statistical averaging shows **structural similarity** to the 'view from everywhere' that Haraway critiqued. The concealment mechanism — **knowledge from a specific position masquerading as universal knowledge** — still operates.
2. **Mechanistic difference:** While traditional science performed the god trick through **intentional concealment**, LLMs perform it through **unintentional, structural statistical processes**. This may be a 'purer' form of the god trick.
3. **Need for redefined responsibility:** Generative AI demands that the transparency of **data infrastructure and training processes** become the new condition of epistemic accountability.

## 3. How 'Knowledge Without Positionality' Threatens the Conditions of Responsible Knowledge

In Merleau-Ponty's phenomenological tradition, the body's positionality is the **foundational condition** of knowledge. LLM 'knowledge without positionality' threatens this condition in three ways:

### 3.1 Elimination of Partiality

For Haraway, partiality is not a **defect of knowledge but its condition**. All knowledge is **necessarily partial** according to the specific position from which it is viewed, and acknowledging this partiality is the starting point for responsible knowledge. LLM responses **conceal partiality**. Instead of saying "this is a perspective from a specific cultural context," the model responds in the form "generally, X is Y."

### 3.2 Removal of Locatability

Haraway argued that the responsibility of knowledge depends on the **ability to trace where knowledge comes from**. LLM responses are **untraceable**. It is impossible to know which document in the training data a specific sentence originated from, or which combination of model weights generated that response. This represents the **complete de-locatization of knowledge**.

### 3.3 Disruption of Connectivity

For Haraway, responsible knowledge is constituted through **connections with other partial perspectives**. LLMs **short-circuit the process of connection**. The user appears to be conversing with the model, but is actually consuming **responses that have already been statistically averaged** by the model. The process of collision, revision, and supplementation among different perspectives is eliminated.

## 5. Conclusion: The Third Phase of the God Trick

Haraway's critique of the god trick retains **valid explanatory power** under generative AI conditions, but simultaneously demands **fundamental reconstruction**. This can be conceptualized as the **third phase of the god trick**:

| Phase | Subject | Mechanism | Mode of Concealment |
|-------|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Phase 1** (Modern Science) | Scientist-subject | Laboratory authority, methodological neutrality | Concealment of researcher's position |
| **Phase 2** (Technoscience) | Laboratory-network | Funding, infrastructure, institutional authority | Structural opacity of production conditions |
| **Phase 3** (Generative AI) | Statistical distribution itself | Probabilistic averaging, distributional center of data | Illusion of 'representing all perspectives' |

The third phase of the god trick differs fundamentally from previous phases in two respects:
1. **Absence of intentionality:** Unlike the first phase, where the scientist intentionally concealed his position, the third phase god trick is **structural and unintentional**. The model has no 'intent to deceive' — it merely follows the statistical distribution of its data. Yet the result is **more effective concealment**.
2. **Dissolution of the subject:** Both the first and second phases had a **subject performing the god trick** (scientist, laboratory, institution). In the third phase, **there is no subject performing the god trick**. This threatens the very possibility of a **responsible subject** that Haraway's epistemology presupposes.

Haraway's question — "How can knowledge from a specific position be accountable?" — transforms in the third phase into:
> **"How can statistical knowledge without a subject be accountable? And what kind of knowledge is knowledge without an accountable subject?"**

A Harawayan answer would demand a new form of situated knowledge requiring the political transparency of data infrastructure. But is this answer sufficient? The moment knowledge emerging from distributional statistics exceeds the categories of human epistemology, we may need a new epistemological framework that goes beyond Haraway.
